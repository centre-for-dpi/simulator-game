# Build the Rails

A browser game about building digital public infrastructure (DPI) and AI, made for CDPI workshops and meetings.

Teams play a country's ten-year DPI and AI journey, either as the national program lead or as CDPI's advisory team. The mission is to reach **one billion new people** with DPI and AI services. Along the way they face procurement and vendor pressure, political fights, budget crunches, crises, elections and AI choices. Every outcome comes with a CDPI lens on the trade-off.

## Play

- Download `index.html` and open it in any modern browser. It is a single self-contained file, with no build step and no server needed. It needs an internet connection only to load its fonts.
- To play in a meeting, project it, let the room debate, and choose with keys 1–4 (or A–D). An optional 45-second vote timer is in the setup.

## What's in the repo

| File | What it is |

| `PRODUCT.md` | Product notes: mechanics, rules, data sources, balance and open questions |

## Data sources

- **DPI Map** (https://dpimap.org/data/), snapshot of 31 March 2026: identity, payment and data exchange status per country.
- **CDPI's DPI Wiki** (https://docs.cdpi.dev/) for concepts such as DaaS, building blocks and the ID Account Mapper.
- **CDPI country engagement status** as of September 2026.
- **IdLAC** (https://redgealc.org/idlac/) for regional cross-border digital identity in Latin America and the Caribbean.

Starting values for most countries are simplified game values, not assessments.
